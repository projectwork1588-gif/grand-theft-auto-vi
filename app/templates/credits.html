Credits and legal

Whose work is whose, and where it comes from.

Unofficial fan project

Grand Theft Auto VI: Team Valkyrie is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project made for study. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Rockstar's artwork and video

Grand Theft Auto, Grand Theft Auto VI, and the names, characters, places, logos, images and videos of the game belong to Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. All rights reserved.

Images and trailers on this site are loaded from rockstargames.com/VI and credited to Rockstar. They are not stored in this project's repository. Facts about the game come from the same site, and each official page links back to where it was found:

  • Videos, screenshots and artwork
  • Only in Leonida, for the regions

The short silent loops in the cast section are cut from the character clips Rockstar publishes on its media page. The full-screen moment on the homepage is nine seconds of Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1, played from Rockstar's own upload in YouTube's embedded player. It is not copied or hosted here, and it is silent unless you turn the sound on. If Rockstar asks for any of this to come down, it will.

Team Valkyrie's own work

The code, layout and written copy are our own and are shared under the MIT licence. The characters, places, stories and vehicles marked Concept are invented by us for this project and are not part of Rockstar's game.
