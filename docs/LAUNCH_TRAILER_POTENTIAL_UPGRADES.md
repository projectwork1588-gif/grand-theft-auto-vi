# Launch Trailer: potential upgrades for Home and Characters

Evidence only. Nothing in Home or Characters was changed, and nothing was cut from the trailer for them. The Garage sea clip is the only excerpt that was made (see `docs/MEDIA_INVENTORY.md`).

**Method.** The Launch Trailer (`media/source/rockstar/gta-vi-launch-trailer.mp4`, 89 s) was stepped through at one frame a second and the candidates were compared, frame against frame, with the Home moment posters (`media/cinematic/moment-1..3.jpg`) and the Characters clips (`media/clips/*.jpg`). Timestamps are good to about plus or minus 1 s: the exact in and out points would be set by stepping frame by frame before any cut. A side-by-side board was made at `review/gate6/trailer/compare.jpg` (Git-ignored); the contact sheets are next to it.

**Verdict: no replacement is justified.** The existing Home moments and Characters clips are as good as, or better than, anything comparable in the Launch Trailer. Two optional additions are listed below; neither is substantial enough to recommend without your decision.

## Home

| Existing | Launch Trailer candidate | Judgement |
|---|---|---|
| Moment 2, "Sun, water, speed" (jet ski, spray, clouds) | 55 to 56.5 s: aerial of a beach with a biplane trailing pink smoke | Not better. Wide and busy, about 2 s long, a stunt aircraft rather than speed on water. The jet-ski frame is more striking and has a clear subject. Do not replace. |
| Moment 3, "Last light" (palms, power lines, bridge at golden hour) | 62.5 to 64 s: a woman on a balcony at sunset | Not a clear upgrade. A fine sunset but the subject is a person, so it belongs to Characters, and the existing frame is a more atmospheric place shot. Do not replace. |
| Moment 1, "Dawn over Leonida" (flamingos) | none | Nothing in the trailer matches the wildlife atmosphere; the iguana shot (21 to 22 s) is 1 s and cut with a car. Keep. |

The trailer's frame is letterboxed (black bars above and below inside the 16:9 file), so any cut for Home would have to be cropped to the picture area first.

## Characters

| Existing | Launch Trailer candidate | Judgement |
|---|---|---|
| Lucia clip: night, neon street, leaning from a car door | 26 to 27.7 s: a daylight two-shot of the woman leaning on a convertible door beside a man at the wheel, sunlit, wind in her hair | Different mood rather than better. The existing night shot suits the page's neon identity; this one is a calmer, brighter alternative. Optional **supplement** (for example the Lucia profile page), not a replacement. |
| Jason clip: shop interior, bandana, glasses | 40 to 42 s: the man and the woman standing against a dark red car, containers and a billboard behind, golden light | A good composed two-shot, stronger than the single-character clip for a "both leads" moment. Optional **supplement** if a duo slot is ever wanted. |

Two cautions on the Characters candidates: the trailer carries no captions in these shots, so the people in them are not named by the trailer itself (their names would have to be confirmed from Rockstar's own material before use). And both shots are letterboxed and about 2 s long, the same length as the existing clips.

## Recommendation

Leave Home and Characters as they are. If you want one addition, the 40 to 42 s two-shot is the best candidate (a supplement for Characters, not a replacement), and it should be cut from the 4K source only after you approve it.
