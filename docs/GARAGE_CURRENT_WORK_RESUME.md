## Status update (camera made visible, 2026-10-18)
Cause of the invisible camera: the owner's computer asks for reduced motion and the server sets `TV_AUTOPLAY_ALWAYS=1`, so `html.motion-on` was set but site.css's blanket reduced-motion rule forced every animation to .001 ms, freezing the camera at its end state. `garage_concept.css` now gives the Garage's own timings back under `html.motion-on` only. The push is 5 to 9 percent per scene (`--zf`), centred on the side of the machine that faces its words. A second defect fixed: the showcase waited on a scene's small glow picture instead of its photograph (the glow was first in the markup), so scenes could appear empty; it now waits for `.gx-art img`, decodes ahead, and trickles the other pictures in one at a time. Hold 2.8 s. `TV_AUTOPLAY_ALWAYS` is ignored by `ProductionConfig` and the page logs a console notice when it is the only reason motion is on. QA: `garage_camera.mjs` measures the running animation, the growth of the picture and the pixel change in both a reduced-motion and a normal browser.
